=== typeset -i (integer attribute) ===
After 'typeset -i n=42': n='42'
After 'n=n+1':          n='43' (integer arithmetic applied)
After 'n="hello"':     n='0' (assigns 0; non-numeric string becomes 0)

=== typeset -r (readonly attribute) ===
After 'typeset -r rovar=immutable': rovar='immutable'
(Attempting 'rovar=change' would cause an error; skipped for safety.)

=== typeset -l (lowercase attribute) ===
After 'typeset -l lc="HELLO WORLD"': lc='hello world'
After 'lc="ANOTHER TEST"':           lc='another test'

=== typeset -u (uppercase attribute) ===
After 'typeset -u uc="hello world"': uc='HELLO WORLD'
After 'uc="another test"':            uc='ANOTHER TEST'

=== typeset -x (export attribute) ===
After 'typeset -x myexport=exported_value'
Variable is exported:
myexport=exported_value

=== typeset -a (indexed array) ===
After 'typeset -a arr=(10 20 30)': arr=(10 20 30)
arr[0]='10' arr[1]='20' arr[2]='30'

=== typeset -A (associative array) ===
After 'typeset -A assoc=([key1]=value1 [key2]=value2)'
assoc[key1]='value1'  assoc[key2]='value2'

=== typeset -n (name reference) ===
After 'typeset -n ref=original':
original='I am the original'
ref='I am the original'
After 'ref="Changed via ref"':
original='Changed via ref'
ref='Changed via ref'

=== typeset -f (display function definition) ===
Output of 'typeset -f myfunc':
myfunc () 
{ 
    echo "Inside myfunc";
    local x=5;
    echo "x=$x"
}

=== typeset -F (list function names) ===
myfunc

=== typeset -t (trace attribute) ===
After 'typeset -t tracetest=traced': (trace attribute set)
tracetest='traced'

After 'typeset -i -r printtest=99':
declare -ir printtest="99"

After 'comb=comb+1':    comb='43' (integer arithmetic active)

After 'comb2=comb2+1': comb2='100' (integer arithmetic active)
